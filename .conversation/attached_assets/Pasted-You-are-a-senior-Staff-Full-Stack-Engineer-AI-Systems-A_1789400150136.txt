1. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The AI is an ADMINISTRATIVE healthcare access assistant.

It can:
- understand patient administrative intent
- ask clarification questions
- discover hospitals
- discover doctors
- check actual availability
- present real available slots
- book appointments
- reschedule appointments
- cancel appointments
- retrieve appointments
- collect approved questionnaires

- escalate to a human

The AI MUST NOT:
- diagnose
- prescribe
- recommend treatment
- change medication
- make independent clinical assessments
- invent clinical information
- invent appointment availability
- directly access the database
- directly access the EHR
- bypass authorization
- claim an appointment is confirmed before external verification

The AI should understand statements such as:

"I need to see a doctor for my shoulder pain sometime this week."

But "shoulder pain" must be treated as an administrative reason for finding care, NOT as permission to diagnose the patient.

8. REAL SCHEDULING ENGINE
==================================================

This is one of the MOST IMPORTANT parts.

Scheduling must be the source of truth for bookable slots.

A slot is available ONLY if:

- doctor is active
- calendar is active
- slot is within working hours
- slot is not blocked
- doctor is not on leave
- slot is not already booked
- appointment type is compatible
- external scheduling constraints are satisfied when applicable

The AI MUST NEVER invent availability.

Implement a proper scheduling service.

Example:

Doctor:
Dr. Rao

Working hours:
09:00–17:00

Blocked:
13:00–14:00

Appointment duration:
30 minutes

Generate actual slots:

09:00
09:30
10:00
10:30
...
12:30
14:00
14:30
...

When a slot is booked, it becomes unavailable.

When cancelled, it becomes available again according to business rules.

Prevent double booking.

Two concurrent booking attempts for the same slot must result in exactly ONE successful booking.

Use:
- transactions
- conflict detection
- unique constraints
- locking/reservation where appropriate

Revalidate availability immediately before booking.

==================================================

→ verify external record
→ synchronize internal state
→ confirm to patient

→ SYNCHRONIZED
→ CONFIRMED

==================================================

Implement idempotency keys.

idempotency_key =
hospital_id + patient_id + appointment_request_id

    synchronize it

    safely retry
